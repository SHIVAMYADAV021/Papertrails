About Nikita Yadav

Founder & editor

Nikita Yadav started Paper Trails with one simple belief: kids already have amazing stories inside them, and they just need a place to share them.

She works side by side with young writers, from shaping a first idea to polishing the final page, as she did with Aadhvika on Survival. Then she gets each story in front of readers their own age, for free.

Questions from kids & grown-ups

FAQ

Are the books really free?

Yes! Every story in the library can be read online for free. If you'd like a printed copy, you can buy one from our partner bookstores.

Who writes the books?

Kids do! Every book is written by a young author, with gentle editing help from the Paper Trails team.

How do I get my story published?

Fill in the Write With Us form with a parent or guardian. We read every story, help polish it, and add it to the free library.

Is Paper Trails safe for kids?

Yes. There are no ads and no public comments, every story is checked before it goes live, and submissions need a parent or guardian's email.

What age are the books for?

Most stories suit readers aged 6 to 13. Each book shows a reading age so it's easy to pick the right one.
